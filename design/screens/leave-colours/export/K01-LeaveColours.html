Leave colours

The colours a time-off policy can take in the calendar. Approved by Ulia on 19 Nov 2026. They replace the tag palette's pale fills (SPEC § 4.7) for leave only: on the company calendar, the profile's Time off calendar, Who's out and every policy marker.

Eleven to pick fromA policy picks one of the first eleven. A new policy takes the first colour no other policy uses.
Gray is AwayGray is never offered: it marks leave whose type the viewer may not see.
ApprovedThe fill, the text colour on it. Pill radius full, 28 high on the board.
Waiting for approvalWhite, a 1.5 px dashed edge in the edge colour, the text colour.
MarkerA dot on a tint: a 16 px circle of the fill with an 8 px dot of the edge colour, no edge (replaces the 16 × 6 policy marker). Pending in a legend: a 16 px dashed ring.
