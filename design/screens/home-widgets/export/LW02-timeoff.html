Time off
Your balance, what is booked and pending, and a quick way to ask for more.
Who sees itEveryone with a time-off policy. Without a policy the widget shows the empty state.
DataTime off: balances by type, requests of the person.
Click“My time off” → the My time off page. “Request time off” → the request dialog (L 800).
SizesSmall · Medium · Large
NotesThe main type (first in the policy, usually vacation) is the big number; the other types are a quiet list under a line. Small shows only the main type. Only what is left is shown, never “of N” or a total. A type without a limit says “No limit”. Upcoming: one line per request, just the dates and the status (no type, no day count). Medium shows the nearest request and a “+N” counter if there are more; Large shows up to 3, then “+N more”. Both open My time off. “Request time off” is in every size.
