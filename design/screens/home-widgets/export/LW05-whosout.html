Who’s out
Who is away today or this week: your team first, everyone with one switch.
Who sees itEveryone. People without a team see Everyone by default.
DataTime off and public holidays; approved and pending (pending dashed). Leave type follows the company’s privacy setting (“Away” if types are hidden).
ClickClick a person → their profile. “Calendar” → team calendar.
SizesSmall · Medium · Large
NotesScope: My team / Everyone. Medium and Large show the switch; Small has no room, so it names the scope in the top right (“My team”) and follows the last choice made in Medium or Large. The type of leave is shown only if the company allows it; otherwise “Away”. Sick days never show a reason. Leave that goes on past this week runs off the right edge of the card (no round end, fades out, →) and says when the person is back. When few people are out this week, the bottom of the card lists Upcoming leave (name, type, dates; as many as fit); with nothing upcoming it says “No leave scheduled”. With many people (Everyone) the list scrolls inside the card under the fixed row of days; the card never grows. Hover any bar: a light tooltip with the full name, type and dates (needed for one-day bars where the name is cut).
