Grid4 columns of 254, gap 24, rows of 160 (page content 1088 at 1440). A widget snaps to whole cells.
SizesSmall 1×1 (254 × 160) · Medium 1×2 (254 × 344) · Large 2×2 (532 × 344). Not every widget has every size.
CardWhite, radius 28, padding 24 (Small 20), no border, shadow 0 1px 2px 6% + 0 8px 24px 8% (neutral). → Changes to SPEC: radius and shadow differ from § 13.9.
TypeTitle Inter Tight 20/26 500. Big number Inter Tight 400, 44 (Small) to 96 (Medium countdown). Body 14/20, hints 12/16. → Changes to SPEC: title and numbers bigger than § 5.
ButtonsPrimary in the accent, radius 12, height 40 (36 / 32 inside rows). Approve always with the nod ring.
StatesLoading: skeleton after 2 s. Empty: S illustration (96) + title + one line + max one action. Error: neutral, “Try again”. No access: the widget is not offered in the panel and not shown.
ColourOnly the accent is themed. Statuses always icon + word (SPEC § 4.5). Avatars on soft tag colours, never the accent.
CopyEnglish, sentence case. Every word on the cards goes into the spec.
