For me · Waiting on you
Every kind of card
One card shape for everything that reaches you. What changes: who or what it is from, the main line, and the buttons.
Order in the deck: Decide first (oldest first), then Do (by due date), then Read (newest first).
Every card: ← Skip moves it to the back (this is “later”, so cards never have a separate Later button). Click anywhere else opens its page.
Read cards leave the deck on “Mark as read”; they stay in Inbox. Counter “1 of 9” counts all three kinds.
Buttons: no “Open”: the card itself opens on click. One primary at most (Approve with the nod ring); Decline and the rest secondary. Read-only cards have no primary.
