# Product rules from this round

These are UI rules Ulia set while designing the Company hub. They apply beyond the hub. They are proposals for the owner's notes (`rules/`, then `sync-rules.mjs`). The numbers continue from the Home round.

## Layout
20. **One sidebar.** A section never adds a second sidebar or a rail of its own. Sub-pages are tabs, links or a back link.
21. **The accent band belongs to Home only.** No other page has a coloured header.
22. **No boxes inside boxes.** A page has one hierarchy, and zones are separated by air and hairlines, not nested frames.
23. **Reading and managing are apart.** People who manage something do it on its own screen ("Manage hub"), entered by a quiet button. The reader's page carries no edit controls.
24. **Section headings in two columns sit on one line**, at the same height.

## Words
25. Ask for an agreement with **"agree"**: "Pages need your yes", "I agree", "{n} of {m} agreed". Do not use "confirm" or "acknowledge" for the same act.
26. Name what is late by what happened: **"Missed the deadline"**, "Deadline was 10 Nov", not "overdue people".
27. **"Must read"** is the property of a page. A list of them is "Must-read pages".

## Content
28. **Icons, not emoji**, for posts and pages: Hugeicons, or the post's own image as a thumbnail. An emoji appears only when the author types it at the start of a title.
29. **No read counters** on posts: no "read by", no views.
30. **No version switcher** for readers: they see the current version and the fact of it ("Version 3 · updated 3 Nov").
31. **Celebrations are their own strip**, never mixed into news. "Out today" is not a celebration.
32. **Saved lives on the hub home only**, not repeated on inner pages.
33. **"+" to add a child sits on the parent row**, not on every row and not on the page header.
34. **The page's main create button follows the tab**: New post on Posts, New page on Pages, none on Settings. Overview has both as equal secondary buttons.
35. **Stat tiles are compact:** the label on top, the number, then one line of hint under it.
36. **Something people must do is optional per item**, switched on where it is needed, with a hint about when to leave it off.
