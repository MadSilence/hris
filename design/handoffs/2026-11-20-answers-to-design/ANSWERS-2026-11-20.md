## B. Where our rule stays

### B1. Dates follow the company's country (PE3, and "never 19.11.2026" in documents)
The month is always written as a word, as you asked. The order of day, month and year belongs to the company's
country, because a reader in the US reads "11/19" differently from one in Europe:
- a European company gets exactly your "23 Jul 2021" and "Mon 23 Nov";
- a US company gets "Jul 23, 2021" and "Mon, Nov 23".

Please draw with the European form, as you do. A board in that form is right for every company.

### B2. No print on the calendar
The calendar's printer icon is not built, and the print we have today is removed with everything behind it. Export
stays. Please take the printer icon off the calendar boards and out of the spec.
- ⚠ **What goes:** printing the board, without the sidebar and with every loaded row. Nobody needs it; export covers
  a copy on paper.
