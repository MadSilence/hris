How to use this package:
- `screens/sign-in-appearance/export/*.html` — every board state as HTML (open in Chrome at 1512×982). `images/*.png` — the same at 1×. `spec.md` (with § "Decisions 19 Nov 2026" at the end — it overrides "Built → Differs"), `words.md` — every string.
- `measurements/sign-in-appearance/<state>.md` — every element of every state: position, size, font, colour **with its token**, background, border, radius, padding, gap, shadow, Hugeicons name. Take every number from there. `renders-2x/` — the states at 2×.
- `components/` — each part of the page cropped at 2× with its final values and the board CSS (`components/index.html`).
- `compare/` — the board next to the live product (19 Nov).
- `tools/overlay.html` — load a board PNG and your screenshot: opacity slider and difference mode.

Rules: measurements win over pictures; the spec wins over the export. Toasts have no period; US English; sentence case; never disabled buttons.
