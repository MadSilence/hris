# Step lists — done is a tick, not a colour (Ulia, 20 Nov 2026)

Applies to every list of steps a person goes through:
- who approves a request (AQ04a, AQ04b, AQ04c);
- who signs a document: the signing panel S07, the sign screen G02/G03, the guest page G09, "Next to sign" in G04.

Telling steps apart by colour alone hides the order. With a green company colour, "done" and "now" would both be green.

## Rule
- **Sizes.** Step mark 16, avatar 24; the mark is always smaller than the avatar. Mark and avatar sit on the name's first line.
- **Done.** A 16 circle in Mist #F3F3F5 with an Ink tick (10, stroke 2.5). The status line under the name stays green, status-approved-text #135C3A ("Manager · Approved Fri 20 Nov").
- **Now.** A 16 circle with a 1.5 px accent ring and the step number (600 10/12) in the accent. The name is in the accent; the status in #7A4800 ("Waiting for you").
- **Not yet.** A 16 circle with a 1.5 px #DADADF ring and the number in #6B6B70; the status in #6B6B70.
- **No order (any 2 of 3).** No numbers. Done keeps the tick circle; now is an 8 px accent dot; not yet is an 8 px #DADADF dot. The count sits on the right of the label row: "1 approved" (500, #135C3A).
- **No frame around the list.**
  - A grey label 13/18 #6B6B70 on top ("Who approves", "Who signs").
  - Steps one under another, with a 1 px #EAEAEC line between the marks and 14 between steps.
  - Name 14/20 500, with a line 13/18 under it (role · status).
- **The requester's own view** carries no names of who hasn't decided: "Waiting for approval · 1 of 3 approved" (AQ04c).
- **Declined** stays red.
