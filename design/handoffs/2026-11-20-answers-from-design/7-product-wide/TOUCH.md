# Hover actions on touch screens (AQ-15 — Ulia, 20 Nov 2026)

- **Desktop.** Row actions appear on hover, as drawn (Download on a document row, × on a request).
- **Phone or tablet.** The same actions are always visible.
- **No copies in ⋯.** Never duplicate an action in a ⋯ menu just for touch.
- **Tables** scroll sideways on narrow screens.
