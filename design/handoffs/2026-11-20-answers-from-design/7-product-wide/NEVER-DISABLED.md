# Nothing is shown disabled (AQ-01, DQ-09 — Ulia, 20 Nov 2026)

- **Buttons.** A button is always pressable. If it can't do its job yet, it says why on press: under the field, or in a toast.
- **Menu items.** A menu item that can't work is hidden, not greyed.
- **When the reason must be seen before the click,** put it on an item that is there. Example: the tooltip "You can delete a profile once it’s inactive." sits on **Set to inactive**; Delete profile stays hidden until the person is inactive.
